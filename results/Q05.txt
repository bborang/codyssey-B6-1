Q05 | 조인 | 도서명과 해당 카테고리명을 함께 확인한다.

실행 SQL:
SELECT b.id AS book_id, b.title, c.name AS category_name
FROM book AS b
INNER JOIN category AS c ON b.category_id = c.id
ORDER BY b.id;
